I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at The Pennsylvania State University. I received my M.Eng. in Control Science and Engineering from Hunan University and my B.Eng. in Automation from Fuzhou University.

Research Interests

  • Network Optimization and Learning: Design and analysis of scheduling, routing, and resource allocation algorithms for real-time synchronization in networked systems.

  • Embedded Intelligent Systems: Embedded sensing and control; IoT/XR systems; AI-assisted sensing and adaptive interaction.

Embedded Intelligent Systems

AIMNET: IoT gas sensing network

Built the IoT networking architecture for large-scale gas sensing, integrating an Arduino-compatible Feather M0 controller, a SIM7070G cellular modem, and an AT&T SIM card. Implemented MQTT-based sensor telemetry and remote device configuration between field sensors and a central server.

Cervical rehabilitation system

Developed an STM32 controller for motion sensing and training feedback, including sensor integration, PCB design, board assembly, and firmware.

sEMG-based robotic hand control

Integrated wearable surface electromyography (sEMG) sensing with robotic hand control.
